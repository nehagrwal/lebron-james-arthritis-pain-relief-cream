How does the LeBron James Arthritis Pain Relief Cream work?

A topical arthritis product can work in a variety of ways, depending on the active ingredient. One example is diclofenac sodium topical gel . This is a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug that works by inhibiting the synthesis of prostaglandins , which are involved in pain and inflammation. When applied to the skin it delivers the medication locally rather than relying entirely on an oral dose.

Other topical products work mostly by sensory effects. Menthol and camphor produce cooling or warming effects , changing how pain signals are interpreted . Capsaicin has a different mechanism of action and may reduce some pain signals with repeated use. While these strategies may help make the discomfort easier to cope with, they don’t necessarily address the structural changes that come with arthritis.

The potential value of a product is dependent on diagnosis, location of pain, the active ingredient and correct use. The FDA labelling for diclofenac topical gel clearly specifies certain joints and arthritis pain as appropriate uses, highlighting the reason why consumers should adhere to the label and not assume any pain cream is suitable for foot, back, nerve or sports-related pain.
